How it works

  1. Add HoverTranslate to Chrome, Firefox or Edge.
  2. Open a YouTube video and turn on subtitles — the creator's or auto-generated.
  3. Point at a word: its translation appears above the subtitles, and the video pauses until you move away.

Why HoverTranslate

Keep reading the original

Most subtitle tools translate the whole line or show a second line of subtitles under the first. HoverTranslate does something smaller on purpose: it translates the one word or phrase you point at, so you still read the original and only look up what you actually don't know. No copying words into a translator, no switching tabs, no losing the thread.

FAQ

Questions

Is HoverTranslate free?
Yes: free and open source under the MIT License, with no account, no subscription and no ads. Only DeepL needs your own API key; its free plan covers 500,000 characters a month.
How do I translate a phrase?
Hold Shift and move the pointer across the words, or turn on “Always enable multiple selection” in the settings.
Which languages can it translate?
More than 240 with Google. The subtitle language is detected automatically; you pick the language to translate into.
Does it work on Netflix or other video sites?
No. HoverTranslate works on YouTube: on youtube.com and in YouTube players embedded on other websites. It does not support Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or other platforms.
Does it show dual subtitles or translate the whole subtitle line?
No. It translates the word or phrase under your pointer. The original subtitles stay as they are.
Does it work in YouTube videos embedded on other websites?
Yes, except videos embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).
Where are my saved words, and can I export them?
In your browser, in the extension's own storage. Open the extension's popup and go to Dictionary to see them; you can export them from there as CSV or JSON.
Do I need a DeepL API key?
Only if you want to use DeepL. Google and Bing work without a key. DeepL's free API plan covers 500,000 characters a month; the key is free to create at deepl.com/pro-api.
Which browsers are supported?
Chrome, Firefox and Edge on a computer. There is no mobile version.
Are there known limitations?
Videos embedded in “privacy-enhanced mode” (youtube-nocookie.com) are not supported yet.
